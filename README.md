# George Dharma

I'm an MSc International Finance candidate at IAE Aix-Marseille. My interests centre on valuation and M&A, and I'm pursuing investment banking and corporate finance roles in France.

When I build a financial model, I want to know which assumptions would make me change my decision. My [finance portfolio](https://georgedharma.github.io/) includes a valuation of Legrand and a case study on how DSV financed its acquisition of Schenker.

## Selected Python projects

- [Hedging a euro swap portfolio](https://github.com/GeorgeDharma/interest-rate-curves-and-swap-hedging) tests how much protection a €20m hedge provides across ten interest-rate shocks.
- [Pricing and hedging a European put](https://github.com/GeorgeDharma/options-pricing-and-hedging) compares daily hedging with a delta-based rule that cut average trades from 249 to 84 in the base simulation.
- [Sizing an industry momentum portfolio](https://github.com/GeorgeDharma/industry-momentum-and-portfolio-selection) compares four momentum allocations by their returns after costs and their risk relative to an equal-weighted industry portfolio.
